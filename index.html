About this MSc portfolio project

Public technical case study · v1.1.0

Novel meals without repeating yesterday

NovelBite is a public-safe reconstruction of a more detailed private workflow. It keeps the recommendation, data-pipeline and privacy ideas while replacing restaurant identity, private history and branded assets with fictional data.

The ranking model is heuristic rather than machine-learning based because the personal feedback dataset is deliberately small. Transparency and testability are more useful here than pretending limited data has become prophecy.
